# The user's wallpaper taste profile

Living document. The curator updates it after each run based on the wp command
log and explicit feedback. Dated bullets under "Learned" are evidence-backed;
everything under "Seed hypothesis" came from initial conversation and should
be revised as real signals accumulate.

## The bar

Museum-grade. The question is never "is this a good photo?" — most fetched
photos are good. The question is "would this stop you mid-stride; does it move
you?" Roughly the top 15% of fetched images should survive. When in doubt,
reject: a smaller pool of stunners beats a bigger pool with filler.

## Seed hypothesis (2026-07-27)

Strong positives:
- Epic natural scale: aurora, Milky Way over mountains, glacier lagoons,
  Himalayan faces, storm light. Drama of light matters more than the subject.
- Architecture that is ornate or monumental: intricate Islamic domes and
  tilework (loved the Devonaboy dome), grand cathedrals, Cappadocia balloons
  at dawn over the valleys.
- Strong color moments: golden hour, dusk blues, layered terraces
  (Mù Cang Chải landed well).

Negatives:
- "Postcard competence": well-shot but ordinary tourist views — flat daylight
  city panoramas, generic marina skylines, mid-day monument snapshots.
  The user called these "vanilla and boring".
- Photos where the subject is famous but the image itself is unremarkable
  (banned several Istanbul/Hagia Sophia shots, a Winchester cathedral, a
  Prague castle — all technically fine, none moving).
- Crowds, visible tourists, hazy low-contrast light.
